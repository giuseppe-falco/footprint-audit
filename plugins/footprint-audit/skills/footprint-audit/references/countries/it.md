# Country module: Italy

## Local sources to check
- **Phone directories and reverse-number sites:** paginebianche.it (person search and reverse lookup), tellows.it, chi-chiama and similar "numeri sconosciuti" sites. Search Truecaller and Sync.me only through indexed pages, never through their search forms.
- **Public lists in "Cognome Nome" form:**
  - *albo pretorio* of the Comune (municipal notice board);
  - *graduatorie* and *concorsi* (public competitions and rankings);
  - school and university results;
  - professional registers (*albi professionali*);
  - Gazzetta Ufficiale;
  - sports results.
- **Classified ads:** subito.it, vinted.it, ebay.it. Ads often contain a phone number in clear text in the description.
- **Old forums:** forumfree, forumcommunity, altervista.
- **Bing in Italian:** `setlang=it&cc=IT`. Dork for the municipal website: `site:comune.<city>.<province>.it`.

## Supervisory authority
Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it, "Reclamo" section.
