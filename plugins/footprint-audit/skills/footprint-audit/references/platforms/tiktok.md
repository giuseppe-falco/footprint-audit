# TikTok

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. TikTok's help center (support.tiktok.com) still redirects to a generic support landing page even in a real browser on 2026-11-11 (JavaScript app, article URLs do not resolve), so menu paths below that are marked "(to verify)" come from secondary sources. The EEA privacy policy (updated 2025-11-30) was read in full.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Open `tiktok.com/@<handle>` logged out (some content may need a login prompt dismissed). Public accounts show bio, videos, follower counts; videos are indexed by search engines.
- Search `site:tiktok.com "<name>"` and the handle in a search engine.
- In the app: profile > "..." / share > view as a non-follower is not a built-in feature (to verify).

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Account public or private; old videos, captions, hashtags, pinned posts; videos where others tagged or "stitched/dueted" you.
- Bio links, e-mail/phone shown, linked Instagram/YouTube.
- Whether the account is suggested to people who have your phone number/e-mail in their contacts, and whether you synced your own contacts.
- Comments and likes visibility; who can duet/stitch/download your videos.

## Settings to tighten
- Profile > Menu > Settings and privacy > Privacy > "Private account" on (path from secondary sources, to verify). Private accounts still show profile photo, name and bio.
- Privacy > "Discoverability" > "Suggest your account to others": turn off Contacts, Facebook friends, and "People who open or send you links" (exact labels and defaults vary by region and age, to verify).
- Privacy > "Sync contacts and Facebook friends": off, and remove any synced contacts (to verify).
- Privacy > Downloads, Duet, Stitch: set to off or friends (to verify).

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: Settings and privacy > Account > "Download your data" (older guides use Privacy > Personalization and data; to verify). Choose format (JSON or TXT/HTML), request; it takes a few days and the file stays available about 4 days (secondary sources, to verify). Some data of others is excluded.
- Formal request: for EEA users the controller is TikTok Technology Limited, 10 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin D02 T380, Ireland (joint controller with TikTok Information Technologies UK Limited). Privacy form: tiktok.com/legal/report/privacy. DPO form: tiktok.com/legal/report/DPO. TikTok may ask you to verify your identity. (Controller, address and both forms confirmed on the live EEA/UK/CH policy, last updated 2025-11-30, section "Contact Us"; the policy also says the "Download your data" tool exists, that you can delete your full account in-app, and that data portability is available via the Data Portability API.)

## Remove
- Delete the account: Settings and privacy > Account > "Deactivate or delete account" > Delete account (to verify). The account is deactivated for 30 days, can be restored by logging in, and is then permanently deleted (30 days is described by a TikTok "discover" page and third-party sources; the official help article was not readable, to verify). Download your data first.
- Delete individual videos from the video menu; remove tags from videos of others via the tag/mention menu (to verify).
- Content posted by others about you: report the video (Share > Report > privacy violation), or use the privacy form tiktok.com/legal/report/privacy for GDPR art. 17 requests.
- Contact-based discoverability: the policy says you can object to, and ask to restrict, processing based on legitimate interests, including suggesting your account to others.
