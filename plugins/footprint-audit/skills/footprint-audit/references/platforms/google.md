# Google (account, Maps, YouTube, Search)

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Search your name in a logged-out or private window: Google profile cards, Maps reviews and YouTube channels show up there.
- Maps: open your contributor profile (Maps > profile picture > "Your profile", to verify) and compare it with the logged-out view of the same URL.
- YouTube: open `youtube.com/@<handle>` logged out; check the About tab and the comments you left on public videos.
- Google Account: myaccount.google.com > Personal info > under "Choose what others see" click "Go to About me" shows and controls what others can see (Only you / Anyone per item; name and date of birth can be edited but not removed) (official help page 6304920, read 2026-11-11; the old "People & sharing" wording and aboutme.google.com are superseded).

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Maps: public reviews, ratings, photos, lists, bio, Local Guide level and points are all public; reviews, photos and ratings cannot be posted privately.
- YouTube: channel name/handle, banner, About links, public playlists, subscriptions, old comments and videos. The YouTube name and photo are separate from the Google name and photo.
- Google account profile: name, photo, nickname; "Profile discovery" decides whether people who only have your phone/email can see your name and photo.
- "Results about you": whether your phone, home address or email appear in Search results.
- Linked apps (myaccount.google.com/linkedapps) and Drive/Photos items shared by public link.
- My Activity (myactivity.google.com): Web & App, Location and YouTube history kept on the account.

## Settings to tighten
- Maps app: profile picture > Settings > Location & privacy > restrict your profile (hides contributions from the public profile; Google says reviews, photos and ratings are still always public on the place listing, so delete them to remove them).
- Google Account > People & sharing > About me: remove optional fields; use the shortened name and hide the photo under Profile discovery if offered.
- Results about you: myactivity.google.com/results-about-you > Get started > add name, phone, address, email > turn on notifications.
- My Activity: set auto-delete for Web & App, Location and YouTube history.

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: takeout.google.com. Zip or Tgz, choose size, delivery by email link or to Drive/Dropbox/Box/OneDrive. Minutes to a few days; links expire after about 7 days. Downloading does not delete anything.
- Formal request: for EEA/Swiss users the controller is Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4). Google LLC is the controller for what is indexed in Search and Maps. Contact the data protection office through the privacy troubleshooter form: https://support.google.com/policies/troubleshooter/7575787. The policy names no DPO e-mail address.

## Remove
- Single services: myaccount.google.com/deleteservices (deleting Gmail needs another e-mail address on the account). The account stays.
- Whole account: myaccount.google.com/deleteaccount. Google says an account "might be" recoverable for a limited time but gives no number of days (verify before promising any grace period). Download with Takeout first; purchases and subscriptions are lost.
- Maps: Menu > Your contributions > Reviews > More > Delete review.
- YouTube: "hide" is reversible; deleting the channel permanently removes videos, comments, messages, playlists and history; it cannot be done on mobile; path: YouTube Studio > Settings > Channel > Advanced settings > "Remove YouTube content" > "I want to permanently delete my content"; it may take time and thumbnails can linger briefly (official https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/18261435). The Google Account is not deleted. The Google Account is not deleted.
- Content by others about you: Search "Results about you" > To review > Request to remove (phone, address, e-mail only; not for news or government sites; the source page stays online). For other content use the EU right-to-be-forgotten delisting form (URL, relation to you, search query, e-mail): https://support.google.com/legal/answer/10769224. Maps/YouTube content by others: report from the item itself.
- Data protection complaints: national authority (Garante in Italy; Irish DPC is lead authority for Google Ireland).
