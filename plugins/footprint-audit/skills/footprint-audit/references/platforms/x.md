# X (Twitter)

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. Checked in a real browser on 2026-11-11 against the live privacy policy (effective 2026-01-15) and help pages; items still marked "(to verify)" were not seen on an official page.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Open x.com/<handle> in a logged-out/private window. Public accounts are visible with limited scrolling; protected accounts show only name, bio and photo.
- No built-in "view as" feature (not found).
- Search engines index public posts and profiles; protecting your posts removes future indexing, not what was already cached.

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Old posts, replies, media, likes (likes are private since 2024, to verify), lists you appear in, bookmarks.
- Bio, location, website, birth date visibility, pinned post.
- Posts where you are tagged in photos or mentioned; communities and Spaces.
- Discoverability by email/phone and uploaded contacts.
- Connected apps (Settings > Security and account access > Apps and sessions); Grok/AI data-sharing toggles (to verify).
- Old handles and alt accounts.

## Settings to tighten
- Settings and privacy > Privacy and safety > **Audience, media and tagging** > "Protect your posts" (optional), "Photo tagging" > off or "Only people you follow".
- Settings and privacy > Privacy and safety > **Discoverability and contacts** > turn off "Let others find you by your email address" and "...by your phone number"; "Remove all contacts" for previously uploaded address books (turning sync off alone does not delete them).
- Privacy and safety > Mentions: limit who can mention you; Direct Messages: restrict who can message you.
- Privacy and safety > Data sharing and personalization / Grok: turn off optional sharing (to verify labels).

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: Settings and privacy > Account > Your X data > verify identity (code by e-mail/phone) > under "Download your data" next to X, **Request data**. "It may take a few days" to prepare; notification by e-mail/push, then download a .zip (HTML and JSON files) while logged in. Download before deactivating. Official page: https://help.x.com/managing-your-account/how-to-download-your-x-archive (older guides cite "Download an archive of your data" and ~24 h: superseded).
- Formal art. 15 request: per the current X privacy policy, for users in the EU/EFTA/UK the controller is **X Internet Unlimited Company** (confirmed on the live policy, effective 2026-01-15), Attn: Data Protection Officer, One Cumberland Place, Fenian Street, Dublin 2, D02 AX07, Ireland. (The policy page does not say "formerly Twitter International Unlimited Company": that is background knowledge.) Contact route: the "Privacy Policy Inquiries" form https://help.x.com/forms/privacy (policy section "Contact us"), or post to the address above. Older sources show dpo@twitter.com (not in the current policy: treat as stale). Lead supervisory authority named in the policy: Irish DPC. Privacy Center: https://privacy.x.com/. EU page: https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies/european-union.

## Remove
- Deactivate: Settings and privacy > Your account > **Deactivate your account**. Deactivation is the first step to deletion and lasts **30 days**: log in within 30 days to reactivate; after that the account is deleted and the username is no longer associated with it. Sent DMs are deleted when deletion completes (official help page, checked 2026-11-11). Paid subscriptions bought through the app are not cancelled automatically. Download the archive first.
- Remove specific content: delete posts; remove tag from photos; remove yourself from conversations (Remove me from this conversation, to verify).
- Content by others: report from the post/profile menu; private-information report form (up to 5 posts per report) per the Private information policy https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies/personal-information; impersonation: https://help.x.com/safety-and-security/report-x-impersonation (no account required per help text, to verify); EU illegal-content (DSA) notice form https://help.x.com/forms/dsa/report (linked from https://help.x.com/rules-and-policies/european-union, confirmed).
- Search engines: deactivated or deleted profiles disappear from X search; Google cache requires the outdated-content tool (see references/removal-eu.md).
