# LinkedIn

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Public view: open your public profile URL in a logged-out/private window. Non-members see only what the public profile settings allow (name, headline, sections you left on).
- Settings: Me > View Profile > Edit icon next to **Public profile & URL** (desktop); mobile: profile photo > Settings > Visibility > Edit your public profile. Switch "Your profile's public visibility" off to hide the whole public profile, or toggle individual sections. Search engines (Google, Bing) may take weeks or months to reflect the change; LinkedIn does not control them.
- No official logged-out preview in the help page; use the private-window test.
- Non-member (guest) controls: https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/g/guest-controls.

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Headline, About, current/past positions, education, location, profile photo (who can see it), contact info (email, phone, address, birthday, websites).
- Old posts, articles, comments, recommendations, volunteering, publications; Activity visible to whom.
- Connections list visibility; followers; "Profile viewing options" (private mode leaks less about you as a viewer).
- Discoverability by email/phone and "People also viewed" style suggestions.
- Synced contacts and connected apps/data partners; job-seeker signals ("Open to work" visible to recruiters/all members).
- Company pages or old duplicate/fake profiles using your name.

## Settings to tighten
- Settings & Privacy > Visibility > "Visibility of your profile & network": **Discoverability by email** and **Discoverability by phone number** > Nobody (or 1st-degree for email). Even then, others can still find you by name and organisation through permitted services.
- Visibility > Edit public profile > off, or sections limited (see above).
- Visibility > "Profile viewing options" > private mode (to verify label).
- Data privacy > "Data sharing with partners/others", "Job seeking preferences" (to verify labels); Advertising data > off for tracking toggles.
- Remove synced contacts: Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > "Syncing options" (to verify).

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service (desktop only): Me > Settings & Privacy > Data privacy > "How LinkedIn uses your data" > **Download your data** > select categories > Request archive. Selected categories arrive by email within minutes, the complete archive within 24 hours; the link stays active 72 hours. Format not stated on the page. EU/EEA/CH members can also use the Member Portability APIs.
- Formal request: controller for members in the EU/EEA/Switzerland is **LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company**. DPO route: online form https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/ask/TSO-DPO (no DPO email or postal address in the privacy policy). Access/portability: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1339364; objection/restriction: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1338610; correction: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1336621. Supervisory authority: Irish DPC. EU-specific rights: https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy/eu.

## Remove
- Erase data: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1342613 (delete specific data, close the account, or use the "LinkedIn Data Deletion Form" for data not listed).
- Close account: Me > Settings & Privacy > Account preferences > Account management > **Change** next to Close account > reason > Next > password > Close account (official https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/63). The official page says you can reopen "in most cases if it's been closed less than **14 days**" (the third-party "20 days" is not confirmed); endorsements, recommendations, pending invitations, followings and group memberships are not recovered. Search engines may still show your info temporarily. Alternative: Hibernate account (Settings & Privacy > Account management > Hibernate account): profile hidden from everyone and from search, posts/messages shown as "A LinkedIn member", reactivation possible after 24 hours (official https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/118565). Public profile and search-engine snippets can persist until re-crawled.
- Individual content: delete the post/comment/article; ask others to remove tags/mentions or report the post (... > Report).
- Content or profiles by others about you: report the profile (... > Report/Block > fake profile / impersonation) or use the Help Center "Report a fake profile"/"Report inappropriate content" forms (to verify URLs); GDPR objection via a1338610 above.
- Search engines: if the public profile is hidden but still in results, use Google's outdated-content tool and Bing's removal form (see references/removal-eu.md).
