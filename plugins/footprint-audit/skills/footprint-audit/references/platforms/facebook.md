# Facebook

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. Official help pages and the Meta privacy policy were read in a real browser on 2026-11-11; items still marked "(to verify)" were not confirmed on an official page.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Profile > **Options** (below your name, on a computer) > **View As** shows the profile as people you are not friends with see it; "Exit View As" in the banner leaves it. Hidden posts and photos stay visible to their original audience elsewhere (Feed, search). Official: https://www.facebook.com/help/288066747875915.
- Also open your profile URL in a private/logged-out browser window: the only reliable test of what a search engine and a stranger see.
- Do not use third-party "profile viewer" tools: they are scams or security risks.

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Old posts, photos and albums: check the audience selector on each (Public, Friends of friends); use "Limit past posts" (to verify path).
- Content where you are tagged by others: Activity Log > "Tags"/"Photos you're tagged in"; remove tags or ask the poster to delete. Tagged content stays visible to the poster's audience in Feed and search.
- About/Intro fields: workplace, education, city, phone, email, birthday, relationship, family members.
- Who can look you up by phone number / email, and whether search engines may link to your profile (see below).
- Contact upload (Settings > "Upload contacts") and apps/websites logged in with Facebook (Settings > "Apps and websites").
- Pages, groups, comments and public events where your name appears.

## Settings to tighten
- Settings & privacy > Settings > Audience and visibility > "How people find and contact you": "Who can look you up using the email address / phone number you provided" > Friends or Only me (wording not re-checked, to verify); "Do you want search engines outside of Facebook to link to your profile?" > Off. Official path: Settings & privacy > Settings > Audience and visibility > **How people find and contact you** (page 124518907626945). Turning it off does not remove content already indexed, nor public posts and comments on Pages and public groups.
- Privacy > "Your activity": "Who can see your future posts" > Friends; "Limit past posts".
- Settings > Audience and visibility > **Profile and tagging**: "Review tags people add to your own posts before the tags appear on Facebook?" > On (official page 247746261926036) and "Review posts you're tagged in before the post appears on your profile?" > On (page 168229546579373; people you are not friends with are prompted to review anyway).
- Public profile details (profile photo, cover, name, username, gender, networks) are always public: pick a neutral photo.

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > **Download your information** (or "Export your information"), choose Facebook, format HTML or JSON, date range, quality. The file stays in "Available downloads" for **4 days** after it is ready (official help page 212802592074644); preparation time is not stated on the page (hours to days per third-party guides). Official path now: Profile picture > Settings & privacy > Settings > **Meta Account** (or Accounts Center) > Your information and permissions > **Export your information** > Create export > choose profile > Export to device or external service > Start export. Notification by e-mail and on Facebook; password-protected.
- Formal art. 15 request: controller for users in the EU is **Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd**, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland (ATTN Privacy Operations). Confirmed on the live policy https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/ ("How to contact Meta"): controller Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, ATTN: Privacy Operations, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5. Both the "contact online" link and the "Contact the Data Protection Officer" link point to the same form, https://help.meta.com/support/privacy/ (reached through a facebook.com redirect). Privacy Center: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/center. Supervisory authority: Irish Data Protection Commission; complaints can also go to the user's local authority.
- Meta's Controller Addendum states data subjects can make art. 15-20 requests directly to Meta Ireland.

## Remove
- Delete vs deactivate: Settings & privacy > Settings > Accounts Center (or Meta Account) > Personal details > Account ownership and control > **Deactivation or deletion** > choose the profile. Deactivation is reversible and keeps data (some info such as sent messages stays visible). Deletion: cancel by logging in within **30 days** (the page adds "the number of days that you can cancel an account deletion varies by region"); after that the account is permanently deleted; removing everything you posted can take up to **90 days**, with copies possibly remaining in backups (official help page 154908788002686, read 2026-11-11). Friends may still see messages you sent.
- Download your data before deleting.
- Content by others about you: report the post/photo (menu "..." > Find support or report); privacy-rights and impersonation forms are in the Help Center (https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/ ... exact form IDs to verify). In the EU, a DSA illegal-content notice form also exists (to verify).
- Search engines: after deletion, content can linger in Google cache; use the search-engine removal tools (see references/removal-eu.md).
