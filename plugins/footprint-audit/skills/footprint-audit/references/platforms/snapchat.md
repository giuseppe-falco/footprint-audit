# Snapchat

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. Snapchat renamed Quick Add to "Find Friends" (see sources); older guides still say Quick Add.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Normal accounts have no public web page. A Public Profile (opt-in, mainly Creator accounts) exposes photo, bio, location, Stories, Lenses, subscriber count, website and public e-mail, and can be found outside Snapchat, for example on the web.
- Check `snapchat.com/add/<username>` and `snapchat.com/@<username>` logged out; also search the username in a search engine.
- From a second account of a friend (with consent) check what a non-friend sees: Bitmoji, display name, Snap Score, Stories set to Public.

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Username (visible to the public), display name and Bitmoji.
- Whether you have a Public Profile or Creator account and public Stories/Spotlight posts.
- Phone number/e-mail linked to the account and whether people can find you with them.
- Contacts sync (uploaded address book), "Find Friends" visibility, Snap Map sharing, Story visibility, who can contact you.
- Memories and My Eyes Only content.

## Settings to tighten
- Settings > Privacy Controls > "Find Friends" (older: "See Me in Quick Add"): off, to stop appearing in suggestions. Official article: tap "Find Friends" under "Privacy Controls" in Settings to control whether you show up in suggestions; "All users can turn off showing up in Find Friends"; formerly called Quick Add; teens appear only in limited cases (https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35382503314580).
- Settings > Privacy Controls > "Contact Me": "Friends" only ("Everyone" is no longer offered except for Creator/business accounts).
- Settings > Mobile Number / Email > turn off "Let others find me using my mobile number/e-mail" (secondary source; to verify). Also Settings > Contacts > "Sync Contacts" off and remove synced contacts (to verify).
- Snap Map: open Snap Map > gear > choose Ghost Mode or selected friends only. Ghost Mode does not stop Snapchat itself from seeing your location, and a Snap submitted to a location-based Story can still appear (secondary source).
- Settings > "Who Can View My Story": friends or custom.

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: accounts.snapchat.com > "My Data", or app Settings > My Data. Pick categories and date range, confirm e-mail, submit. Result is a zip with an index.html; Snapchat aims for 7 days, large exports may take longer; requests cannot be cancelled.
- Formal request: Snap Inc. is the controller for EEA/UK users; Snap B.V. (Keizersgracht 165, 1016 DP Amsterdam) is its EEA representative, not the controller. DPO address is published in the EEA/UK privacy notice (dpo at snap dot com). Contact via the help.snapchat.com request form for privacy questions. Complaints: your national DPA.

## Remove
- Delete the account: accounts.snapchat.com > "Delete my account" (or app Settings > Account Actions > Delete Account), enter password. The account is deactivated for 30 days and can be reactivated in that time, then permanently deleted (India: 180 days). Data download is available during this window. Some records are kept for legal, security and business reasons.
- Remove specific content: delete Memories/Stories/Spotlight posts in the app; Snaps and Chats are deleted from servers within 24 hours after opening or expiry by default (policy; Snap makes no promise of a specific deletion time).
- Contacts you uploaded stay with Snap until you remove them in settings; turning off the device permission may not remove them.
- Content posted by others about you: report in app (long-press > Report); for GDPR art. 17/21 requests use the help.snapchat.com privacy request form.
