# Discord

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. Help articles and the privacy policy (effective 2025-09-29) were read in a real browser on 2026-11-11; "(to verify)" marks what is still unconfirmed.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Username, avatar, banner, bio ("About me"), pronouns and display name are visible to anyone who shares a server with you or opens your profile. Connected accounts shown on the profile (GitHub, Steam, etc.) are visible too.
- Messages in public servers (Server Discovery or public invite links) may be readable by anyone; some public servers are indexed or archived by third-party sites and bots.
- Check by joining nothing: search your username/handle in a search engine and in Discord-lookup sites; do not log in to other servers to snoop on others.

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Your servers list, including servers you forgot; for each, what you posted. Messages stay in a server after you leave it (the policy says you can delete a message only "if you still have access to the space where you posted it").
- Profile: bio, banner, pronouns, connected accounts (User Settings > Connections), status.
- Servers where you are owner/admin (must transfer or delete before deleting the account: confirmed in the official delete-account article).
- Phone/e-mail on the account and "Allow friends found through my contacts" / contact sync.
- Authorized apps and bots (User Settings > Authorized Apps).

## Settings to tighten
- User Settings > Content & Social > Social permissions: toggle off "Direct messages" (default: anyone in a shared server can DM you; you are asked whether to apply it to existing servers). User Settings > Content & Social > Friend Requests: choose among Everyone, Friends of Friends, Server Members (or deselect all). Optional data-use toggles (e.g. "Use data to improve Discord") are under Data & Privacy (labels not re-checked, to verify). Official: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/217916488-Blocking-Privacy-Settings.
- User Settings > Connections: remove accounts you do not want public.
- Contact sync: disable phone and contact sync in User Settings (exact label not found on the pages read, to verify).
- Per server: right-click server > Privacy Settings, and set a per-server nickname/avatar that is not your real identity.

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: User Settings > Data & Privacy > "Request Data" (desktop, web and mobile). The package is a ZIP of JSON files sent to the account e-mail within 30 days; the download link stays active 30 days; disabling or deleting the account before you receive the link cancels the request (official article "Your Discord Data Package", read 2026-11-11). Menu: User Settings > Data & Privacy > "Request your data" > Request Data.
- Formal request: controller for EEA users is Discord Netherlands BV, Schiphol Boulevard 195, 1118 BG Schiphol, Netherlands. Contact privacy@discord.com; DPO dpo@discord.com. UK: representative VeraSafe.

## Remove
- Messages: delete one by one in each server or DM (you must still have access). There is no official bulk-delete tool; third-party "message cleaner" tools use user tokens and are against Discord's terms, so do not recommend them. Messages remain after leaving a server.
- Account deletion: User Settings > My Account > "Delete Account" (or "Disable Account"); you must first transfer or delete servers you own, then enter password (and MFA). Official help article: "pending deletion" for **15 days** ("deletion takes 15 days to process"); restoring the account within that window cancels it; after that it is irreversible. The retention article says the account is first put on hold "for 15 to 30 days", then identifying information is deleted/anonymised, up to 45 days for backups. The account is "anonymized across all servers and messages", i.e. messages stay but are no longer tied to you; no article says they are removed. So delete messages before deleting the account. Accounts inactive for 2+ years are also scheduled for deletion.
- Content posted by others about you: report to Discord Trust & Safety from the message menu; for GDPR art. 17 requests write to privacy@discord.com (identify the servers/messages) and cc the DPO. Note that Discord may be legally required to keep some data.
- Complaints: national DPA. The policy does not name a lead authority; the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens would be the usual lead for Discord Netherlands BV (inference, to verify).
