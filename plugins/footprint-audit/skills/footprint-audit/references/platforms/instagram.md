# Instagram

Last verified: 2026-11-11. Links and menu paths change: re-check before proposing them. Instagram help pages were read in a real browser on 2026-11-11; items still marked "(to verify)" were not confirmed on an official page.

## See yourself as a stranger
- Open your profile URL (instagram.com/<handle>) in a logged-out/private window. Public accounts show bio, posts, reels, follower counts; the login wall limits browsing but not the first posts and metadata.
- No built-in "view as" feature (not found in sources).
- Search engines: Instagram allows Google/Bing to index photos and videos from public reels and posts made since 2020-01-01 by accounts that are all of: holder over 18, public, **professional** account (stories are not indexed). Opt out by turning off the setting in account privacy settings, setting the account to private, or switching from professional to personal; de-indexing takes time, and username, profile picture and bio may still appear (official, https://help.instagram.com/147542625391305).

## What to check (logged in, in the user's own session)
- Account privacy: public vs private; professional/creator accounts are always public.
- Bio, name, username, links, contact buttons (email/phone on professional accounts), linked Facebook Page.
- Photos/reels you are tagged in by others (Profile > Tagged tab); mentions; comments you left on public posts.
- Followers list and Close Friends; story archive and highlights.
- Contact syncing and "Accounts Center" linked accounts (Facebook, Threads).
- Old accounts and the same handle on Threads.

## Settings to tighten
- More > Settings > **Account privacy** (under "Who can see your content") > toggle **Private account** > Switch to private. Business profiles cannot be private: switch to personal first. Under-18 accounts are private by default (official, https://help.instagram.com/448523408565555).
- Settings > Tags and mentions: "Allow tags from" and "Allow mentions from" > People you follow / No one; "Manually approve tags" > On (snippet: Privacy > @Mentions offers Everyone / People you follow / No one).
- Account privacy settings: turn off the option that lets public photos and videos appear in search engines (only shown for eligible public professional accounts; exact toggle label not seen, to verify); otherwise go private or personal (see above).
- Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > "Upload contacts" > off, and delete uploaded contacts (to verify).
- Settings > Followers and content > "Hide story from" and "Activity status" > off.

## Get your data (export / GDPR art. 15)
- Self-service: Settings > Accounts Center > Your information and permissions > **Download your information** (select Instagram, format HTML or JSON, date range). Official path: More > Settings > Meta Account (Accounts Center) > Your information and permissions > **Export your information** > Create export > pick profile > Export to device or external service > Start export. The link stays in "Available downloads" for **4 days**; the page says it "may take up to 30 days" for the export link to be e-mailed.
- Formal art. 15 request: controller for EU users is **Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd**, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland; use the Privacy Center / DPO contact form (see facebook.md). Complaints to the Irish DPC or the local authority.

## Remove
- Delete vs deactivate: Accounts Center > Personal details (or Profiles and personal details) > Account ownership and control > **Deactivation or deletion** > choose the account > Delete account > Continue. After **30 days** from the request the profile and all information are permanently deleted (the page adds that the number of days you can cancel varies by region; during the 30 days content is not visible to others); full completion can take up to **90 days**, with copies possibly in backups; deletion does not delete the Facebook account. After deletion you can sign up again with the same username if no one else has taken it (official, https://help.instagram.com/370452623149242).
- Deactivation (temporary) hides profile; data kept.
- Tags: Tagged tab > open photo > Remove tag (hide from profile) .
- Content by others / impersonation: "..." on the post or profile > Report > pretending to be someone / privacy; Help Center impersonation page https://help.instagram.com/370054663112398 confirms you can report impersonation without an Instagram account (via the contact form; a photo of a government ID is requested; only the impersonated person or a representative can report). Intimate or privacy-rights requests: Help Center privacy forms (to verify).
- Search engines: after changes, request refresh/removal in Google (see references/removal-eu.md).
